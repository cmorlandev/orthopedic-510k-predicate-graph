=== SECOND FAILURE MODE: "REFERENCE DEVICE" SECTIONS (found by auditing K190123) ===

Auditing K190123 against its source PDF corrected an earlier reading of this
verification. Its five SECTION_HEADED false positives do NOT come from the
compatibility tables. They come from a section headed "V. Reference Device:".

In FDA 510(k) terminology a REFERENCE device is a formally distinct category
from a PREDICATE: cited for supporting data, test methods, standards or
labelling precedent, carrying no substantial-equivalence claim. K190123's
section V names five (K932222, K173898, K101575, K842559, K120599); the
extractor's heading detector treated the section as a predicate cue and
emitted all five as SECTION_HEADED edges.

So there are two distinct mechanisms, not one:
  1. reference-device sections  -> contaminate SECTION_HEADED (high-confidence)
  2. compatibility tables       -> contaminate PROXIMITY_ONLY

Mechanism 1 is the more consequential. Compatibility-table documents are rare
(0.31% detected). Reference-device sections are a standard element of modern
510(k) summaries.

--- F2. THE REFERENCE-DEVICE EDGES ARE THE LOCKED RULE, NOT A DEFECT

§5, verbatim: "Predicate cue: a case-insensitive match of predicate,
substantially equivalent, or REFERENCE DEVICE in the summary text."
§8, verbatim: precision is "the proportion of hand-adjudicated candidate edges
that are genuine PREDICATE/REFERENCE citations".

So "reference device" is a preregistered cue and reference citations count as
genuine under the preregistered precision definition. The 1,136 reference-
section edges (999 SECTION_HEADED) are the plan working as written. The
earlier characterisation of them as a failure mode was wrong.

--- F3. NEITHER IS THE COMPATIBILITY-TABLE BEHAVIOUR. THE RULE IS MAXIMAL.

§5, verbatim: "Predicate edge (deterministic rule): in any summary containing
a predicate cue, EVERY OTHER in-corpus K-number present in the text is
recorded as an incoming predicate edge for that device."

Every K-number anywhere in a document that mentions predicates anywhere
becomes an edge. Compatibility tables, accessory lists, reference sections,
standards discussions -- all of it, by design.

--- F4. "SECTION_HEADED" DOES NOT MEAN WHAT ITS NAME SAYS

§5: "SECTION_HEADED if a cue occurs within 300 characters of the K-number's
first mention; otherwise PROXIMITY_ONLY."

Both tiers are proximity rules. They differ only in window width -- there is
no section-structure detection anywhere in the pipeline. This explains K190123
exactly: the heading "V. Reference Device:" is itself a cue, so the five
K-numbers beneath it fall within 300 characters and are labelled
SECTION_HEADED, while the distant compatibility-table entries are
PROXIMITY_ONLY.

The name will mislead readers of the manuscript, and it undercuts what
sens_high_conf can be claimed to demonstrate: it is a tighter-proximity
subset, not a structurally-verified one.

--- F5. THE REAL FINDING IS CONSTRUCT VALIDITY, NOT IMPLEMENTATION

The hypotheses in §4 are about substantial-equivalence lineage: recalled
devices "cited as predicates" by later devices, persistence of predicate
citations, downstream devices, chain depth. The §5 operationalisation counts
co-occurrence of a K-number in a document mentioning predicates. Those are not
the same construct, and the gap is measurable -- which is what the hand
adjudication measured:

  criterion                                          precision   Wilson 95% CI
  manual §9, "genuinely cited as predicates"         162/199  81.4%  75.4-86.2%
  prereg §8, "predicate/reference" (K190123 only)    167/199  83.9%  78.2-88.4%
  prereg §8, if K233507's 4 are also reference       171/199  85.9%  80.4-90.1%

Even on the most generous preregistered reading, 28 of 199 edges are neither
predicate nor reference citations -- they are compatible-component entries.

So the study can defend its numbers as faithful to its plan, and cannot
defend the plan as measuring predicate lineage. Roughly one edge in six is
not a substantial-equivalence citation. This belongs in the manuscript as a
construct-validity limitation with the measured precision attached, not as a
bug report.

  RECOMMENDATION: restrict the edge rule to K-numbers appearing within a
  predicate or substantial-equivalence context, excluding reference-device
  sections and compatibility/accessory enumerations; report the current graph
  as a sensitivity analysis. Failing that, rename the object throughout --
  "predicate-or-reference citation graph" -- and restate the hypotheses in
  those terms.

--- F6. THE ANALYST'S 121/121 IS NOW FULLY EXPLAINED, WITH NO ANOMALY

Under §8's own criterion, reference citations are genuine, so a sample drawn
from the 8.7% of documents carrying reference sections yields YES verdicts.
Compatibility-table documents are rare (0.31-0.90%), so a 60-device draw very
probably contained none. 121/121 is the expected result of applying the
preregistered criterion to a typical draw. Earlier working figures in these
notes that treated it as improbable (1 in 66, then 1 in 3) were both wrong in
premise: there is nothing to explain.
  Two earlier speculations are formally withdrawn: that the analyst may have
  adjudicated loosely, and that their draw was unusually lucky. Neither is
  needed and neither should appear in the report.

--- F7. §2 ASSERTS THE PDFs ARE STATIC. THEY ARE NOT.

§2: "Summary PDFs (the only place predicate relationships are written) are
downloaded separately from accessdata.fda.gov; they are static documents and
are treated as part of the frozen snapshot by retrieval date."

This is the definitive resolution of the 8-field mismatch. The preregistration
discloses that the PDFs sit outside the frozen snapshot, but asserts they are
static. This verification demonstrates they are not: an 18-day gap between the
analyst's Stage 2 and this one produced +11 edges and moved 8 of 20 reference
fields, all in the positive direction, with node counts unchanged. Direct
corroboration was observed during adjudication, when accessdata.fda.gov
returned application errors for a valid K-number.

"Treated as part of the frozen snapshot by retrieval date" means exact
reproduction requires the same retrieval date, which no later verifier can
have. The 8 differing fields are therefore not reproducible by construction --
a property of the study's design, disclosed but mischaracterised, and not a
defect in this rebuild.

  RECOMMENDATION: freeze the extracted PDF text (or the PDFs) into the
  snapshot bundle, or state the affected values as tolerance-bounded. As
  written, every future verifier hits this same 8-field mismatch.

--- F8. THE VALIDATION SAMPLE IS PREREGISTERED AS NON-REPRODUCIBLE

§8: "60 devices drawn with a fixed seed (42) from devices with downloaded
text." The population sampled from is itself run-dependent, so the fixed seed
does not reproduce the sample across runs. This confirms the earlier finding
and locates it in the plan rather than the code: 199 candidate edges over 45
edge-bearing devices here, against the analyst's 121.

--- F9. §9's INDEPENDENCE OPTION WAS PRE-SPECIFIED AND NOT RUN

§3.1-3.2 disclose that hypotheses H1-H4 were derived from a 9-code pilot whose
devices are ~35% of this corpus, making the confirmatory analysis an extension
rather than a replication. §9 offers the remedy -- reporting on the 145
non-pilot codes only -- and pre-specifies it so it cannot be presented later
as a post-hoc discovery. No such field exists in expected_values_REFERENCE.json
and no result appears in RUN_LOG.md. It is labelled optional, so skipping it is
not a deviation, but the one measure offered for independence was not taken and
that should be stated where the pilot overlap is disclosed.

--- F13. TWO ANALYTIC OBSERVATIONS

(a) sens_class_I_II restricts to Class I+II = 1,317 devices, silently dropping
    the 356 recalled devices carrying no severity class (21.1% of 1,691). And
    "Class I/II" is in practice Class II: 1,306 of 1,317 (99.2%). A reader may
    assume the most severe category carries the signal; Class I contributes 11
    devices.
(b) The H4 comparison carries a 35-point differential linkage gap between arms
    (84% recalled vs 49% matched). At 49% control matching, at least 51% of
    controls score zero events because they could not be linked at all, so a
    control median of exactly 0 is what non-linkage alone produces. §10
    declares this risk in advance; the exactly-reproduced match rates quantify
    it and show it is structural rather than noise.

--- F14. 08_verify CANNOT FAIL

Its "expected" values are read from the file 06_graph_and_analysis writes in
the same run, so it is a self-consistency check of the rebuild against itself.
It cannot detect divergence from the analyst, and its 15/15 pass should not be
presented as independent confirmation. The real comparison is
compare_to_reference.py. The verifier manual's framing of this pass as a
meaningful gate is misleading.

=== SECOND ADJUDICATION: REFERENCE-SECTION EDGES (22 edges, 12 documents) ===

A second, independent hand sample drawn from reference_device_edges.csv --
12 documents, 22 edges, all adjudicated. This calibrates the 1,136 upper
bound rather than testing for a defect, since §5 makes reference citations a
locked part of the edge rule.

Question per row: is this K-number under a reference-device heading only, or
also under a predicate / substantial-equivalence heading?
  NO  = reference-only (my scan's attribution was right)
  YES = also a genuine predicate (my scan over-attributed)

  tier              edges   YES   NO   reference-only      Wilson 95% CI
  SECTION_HEADED       17     4   13        76.5%           52.7-90.4%
  PROXIMITY_ONLY        5     2    3        60.0%           23.1-88.2%
  TOTAL                22     6   16        72.7%           51.8-86.8%

Per-document: 8 of 12 documents were entirely reference-only; 4 were mixed.
No document was entirely YES.

CALIBRATED RESULT
  all reference-attributed edges  1,136 upper bound -> ~826  (588-986)
  SECTION_HEADED subset             999 upper bound -> ~763  (526-903)
  => ~826 edges (2.71% of the graph) are reference-only citations, of which
     ~763 sit in the high-confidence tier (3.38% of it).

THE 6 YESes MEASURE A KNOWN WEAKNESS IN MY SCAN
  The scan excluded a K-number when it also appeared in a DETECTED predicate
  section, but only 59.5% of documents had a detectable predicate heading.
  Those 6 of 22 (27%) are precisely that gap -- now measured rather than
  assumed. The scan over-attributes by roughly a quarter, and the calibrated
  figure above corrects for it.

NO DOUBLE COUNTING WITH THE EARLIER ESTIMATE
  non-predicate edges, K-load calibrated        ~2,064   6.8%
    of which reference-only                       ~826   2.7%
    remainder (compatibility enumerations etc.) ~1,238   4.1%
  The 199-edge sample's ratio was 9 reference : 28 compatibility (24:76);
  the corpus ratio is nearer 40:60. That shift is expected, because the
  45-device draw over-represented compatibility documents about sevenfold
  relative to their corpus prevalence.

LIMITATION
  22 edges over 12 documents is a small sample and the intervals are wide
  (51.8-86.8%). Both samples were adjudicated by the same single reader, so
  this is not an inter-rater check. It is a second, independently drawn
  sample from a different population, which is worth more than enlarging the
  first, but it does not address the absence of a second adjudicator.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE MANUSCRIPT
  These ~826 edges are VALID under the preregistered §8 criterion ("genuine
  predicate/reference citations") and INVALID under the construct the
  hypotheses describe (substantial-equivalence lineage). They are therefore
  the clearest available measure of the construct gap in F5: not an error
  rate, but the size of the mismatch between what was locked and what is
  claimed. A single sentence in the manuscript -- "approximately 2.7% of
  edges, and 3.4% of high-confidence edges, are FDA reference-device
  citations rather than predicate citations" -- would discharge it.
